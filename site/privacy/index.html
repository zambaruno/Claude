LOST Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

LOST is operated by Zambaruno LLC. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use LOST.

Information

LOST may process account information, item photographs, capture time and location, and information submitted in connection with a claim. Authentication may be provided by third-party identity providers. Payment information, when payments are enabled, is processed by the applicable payment provider rather than stored as full payment-card details by LOST.

Use

Information is used to operate the lost-and-found service, associate items with accounts, support claims, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve the service.

Location and photographs

Location associated with a found item may be used to help verify and return the item. Precise coordinates are not intended to be publicly displayed by default. Photographs may be stored to provide the service.

Service providers

LOST may use service providers for hosting, authentication, storage, communications, analytics, advertising, or payments. Data is shared only as needed for those functions and subject to applicable settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the service, security, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information, subject to limited retention where necessary for security, disputes, or legal requirements.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: zambaruno@gmail.com.
